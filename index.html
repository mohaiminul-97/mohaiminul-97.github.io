Research 🔬

  • Domain Knowledge Management for Automated Threat Defense: Methods, Challenges, and Opportunities
    Keywords: Knowledge Graphs, Intrusion Detection, Security Automation, Red-Blue Team Activities
    During my Research Assistant internship at NRC, I worked on acquiring and organizing domain knowledge for knowledge graph representation in the context of intrusion detection and security automation, with a particular focus on red-team and blue-team activities. This work resulted in a literature review paper that has been submitted for publication, co-authored with Dr. Euclides Neto and Dr. Shahrear Iqbal.
  • A Birth-Death-Migration Model for Alignment-Free Phylogeny Estimation using k-mer Frequencies
    Keywords: K-mer, Phylogenic tree, Genetics, Bioinformatics, Alignment-free Method, Birth-death-migration
    Given a collection of species' genetic information, we investigate alignment-free methods for phylogenetic tree reconstruction. In particular, we propose a model of k-mer frequency evolution based on a birth-death-migration process, which can be used to estimate maximum-likelihood phylogenies directly from k-mer frequencies. The thesis was supervised by Atif Hasan Rahman, Associate Professor, CSE, BUET.
